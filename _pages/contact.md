## Get in touch

The quickest way to reach the lab is by email:

**[bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com)**

Source files, repositories and issue discussions are on [GitHub](https://github.com/bi4wms).

### What to write about

- Questions, corrections or errata about the engineering notes and open-hardware projects
- Collaboration on open-source hardware, firmware or documentation
- Enquiries about hardware availability (items are currently listed as **price on request**)
- Licensing questions about design files

### What this site does not do

This is a static documentation site. There are no user accounts, no comment forms and no checkout on this domain. We do not collect or store card or payment details; any purchase is completed through a separately linked provider. Please never send passwords, card numbers or other sensitive credentials by email.

### Response time and languages

This is an independent hardware lab, not a staffed support desk. Messages are answered as time allows, generally within a few working days. Both English and Chinese are welcome.

### Privacy of your message

Your email address is used only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list, shared with advertisers or used for tracking. There is no phone or live support channel; the lab works in China Standard Time (UTC+8), so a reply may arrive outside your local business hours.

For details on how your message and visit data are handled, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## 联系方式

联系实验室最快的方式是发邮件：

**[bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com)**

源文件、代码仓库与问题讨论都在 [GitHub](https://github.com/bi4wms)。

### 适合来信的内容

- 对工程笔记、开源硬件项目的提问、勘误或补充
- 开源硬件、固件或文档方面的协作
- 硬件供货情况咨询（目前商品均标注为**询价**）
- 设计文件的授权问题

### 本站不提供的服务

本站是静态文档站点，没有用户账户、没有评论表单，也不在本域名下结算。我们不收集或保存银行卡等支付信息；如需购买，会通过单独链接的外部结算平台完成。请不要通过邮件发送密码、卡号或其他敏感凭据。

### 回复时间与语言

这是一个独立的个人硬件实验室，不是有专人值守的客服。来信会在时间允许时尽快回复，通常为几个工作日内。中文和英文都可以。

### 来信的隐私

你的邮箱地址只用于回复，不会加入邮件列表、不会提供给广告主，也不会用于追踪。本站没有电话或即时客服渠道；实验室按中国标准时间（UTC+8）工作，回复可能出现在你当地的非工作时间。

关于来信内容与访问数据的处理方式，请见[隐私政策](/privacy/)。
