**Last updated: November 16, 2026**

This policy explains how information is handled on **bi4wmslabs.github.io** (the "Site"), a static documentation site for the bi4wms open-hardware lab. It is written to be readable by visitors and to meet the disclosure requirements of advertising and analytics partners such as Google AdSense and Google Analytics.

## 1. Who we are

The Site is operated by BI4WMS, an independent hardware engineering lab. Contact details are on the [Contact page](/contact/). The Site is published as static pages through GitHub Pages; it has no backend database, no user accounts and no shopping cart of its own.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you send us.** If you email bi4wms@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any details you choose to include. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell or rent this information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

**Information we do not collect.** The Site has no registration, login, newsletter, comment or contact form, and no online checkout. We do not ask for names, phone numbers, postal addresses or payment card details, and we do not store any payment information. Product pages that mention a price on request lead to a separately linked checkout provider; that provider has its own privacy policy.

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages and the content delivery network in front of it) may automatically record technical data when a page or image is requested, such as IP address, browser and device type, referring page, requested URL and time of the request. These logs are used to deliver the Site, maintain security and investigate abuse. They are controlled by the hosting provider, not by us; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**Analytics (Google Analytics 4).** We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID `G-3N2K440QKC`) to understand which engineering notes are read, how visitors find the Site and which pages need better documentation. It is loaded only on the published site, never in local previews. With your consent, GA4 sets the `_ga` and `_ga_*` cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions and records the page URL, referrer, approximate region, device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised, and we never combine analytics data with names or email addresses. Analytics runs through **Google Consent Mode v2**: `analytics_storage` stays `denied` until you accept, and you can withdraw consent at any time (Section 5). See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell analytics data or use it to build advertising profiles.

## 3. Cookies and third-party advertising

The Site itself does not set cookies for login or sessions; your language choice is saved locally in your browser and is not a cookie. The Site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, including **Google AdSense**, and uses Google Analytics 4 for anonymous statistics as described in Section 2.

| Type | Purpose | Source | How to manage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Necessary | Page delivery, language preference (browser storage) | This Site | Cannot be disabled |
| Analytics | Anonymous usage statistics | Google Analytics 4 | Accept or withdraw via [Cookie Settings](#cookie-settings) |
| Advertising | Personalised ads, only when ads are enabled | Google AdSense / Google | [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads/) |

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this Site and/or to other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve interest-based ads to users based on their visits to this Site and other sites.
- These third parties may read or place cookies in your browser, and may combine information about your visits with data from other sites. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Advertising content is separate from editorial content. Articles, designs and measurements published on this Site are not altered in exchange for advertising.

If advertising is not currently enabled on the Site, no advertising cookies are set by this Site; this section describes what applies once ads are enabled.

## 4. Your choices

- You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads/).
- You can learn about opting out of interest-based advertising from participating third-party networks at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) for European visitors.
- More detail on Google's advertising cookies is available at [Google's advertising privacy page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- You can turn analytics cookies on or off at any time with the [Cookie Settings](#cookie-settings) button below, or install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- To change an earlier consent decision, use the same button: the consent banner reappears and your new choice takes effect immediately.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not prevent the editorial content of this Site from working.

## 5. Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

We use **Google Consent Mode v2** across the Site. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization` all default to `denied`. No analytics or advertising cookies are written until you choose **Accept analytics** in the consent banner; choosing **Essential only** keeps them off. Until you decide, Google Analytics may receive only cookieless, non-identifying pings that cannot be used to identify you.

You can withdraw or change consent at any time through [Cookie Settings](#cookie-settings) or the button in Section 4. If advertising is enabled in future, the same consent mechanism governs personalised ads. The Site's articles, designs and technical content never depend on advertising or analytics cookies.

## 6. Retention and security

Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer the enquiry and maintain a record of technical collaboration, then deleted. The Site holds no user database. Hosting logs are retained according to the hosting provider's policy. Analytics data is aggregated and retained according to the retention setting of our Google Analytics property; we do not export or store individual-level analytics data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send confidential or sensitive credentials by email.

## 7. Children's privacy

The Site is written for engineers and hardware hobbyists and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the message.

## 8. External links and embedded resources

Articles link to external resources such as GitHub repositories, PDF documentation, YouTube thumbnails and image hosts. Those services have their own privacy policies and may collect data when you follow a link or load an embedded resource. We are not responsible for their practices. Google Analytics 4 is the only third-party script loaded by the Site, and it is described in Section 2.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or object to certain processing. Because we hold no user database, such requests normally relate only to email correspondence: write to [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com) from the address you originally used. We respond within 30 days.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes to how analytics or advertising cookies are used will be described here.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this policy: [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com), or use the [Contact page](/contact/). See also the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and [Licensing & Store Policies](/license/).

**最后更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日**

本政策说明 **bi4wmslabs.github.io**（下称「本站」）如何处理信息。本站是 bi4wms 开源硬件实验室的静态文档站点，既面向读者，也用于满足 Google AdSense、Google Analytics 等广告与分析合作方要求的信息披露。

## 1. 我们是谁

本站由独立硬件工程实验室 BI4WMS 运营，联系方式见[联系页面](/contact/)。本站以静态页面方式通过 GitHub Pages 发布，没有后端数据库、没有用户账户，也没有自建购物车。

## 2. 我们收集哪些信息

**你主动发来的信息。** 如果你向 bi4wms@gmail.com 发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容以及你主动提供的其他信息。这些仅用于回复你和保留沟通记录，不会出售或出租，也不会用于构建广告画像。

**我们不收集的信息。** 本站没有注册、登录、订阅、评论或联系表单，也没有在线结算。我们不会索要姓名、电话、邮寄地址或银行卡信息，也不保存任何支付信息。标注「询价」的商品页会通过单独链接跳转到外部结算平台，该平台有自己的隐私政策。

**服务器日志。** 托管方（GitHub Pages 及其前置内容分发网络）在页面或图片被请求时，可能自动记录技术数据，例如 IP 地址、浏览器与设备类型、来源页面、请求 URL 与请求时间。这些日志用于站点交付、安全维护与滥用排查，由托管方而非我们控制，详见 [GitHub 隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)。

**统计信息（Google Analytics 4）。** 我们使用 Google Analytics 4（衡量 ID `G-3N2K440QKC`）了解哪些工程笔记被阅读、访客如何找到本站、以及哪些页面需要补充文档。该脚本只在正式发布的站点加载，本地预览不会发送数据。在你同意后，GA4 会设置 `_ga` 与 `_ga_*` cookie 用于区分浏览器与会话，并记录页面 URL、来源页面、大致地区、设备与浏览器类型。IP 地址会做匿名化处理，我们也不会把统计信息与姓名或邮箱关联。统计功能通过 **Google 同意模式 v2（Consent Mode v2）** 运行：在你同意前 `analytics_storage` 始终保持 `denied`，并可随时撤回同意（见第 5 节）。详见 [Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) 与 [Google 隐私权政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。我们不会出售统计数据，也不会用它构建广告画像。

## 3. Cookie 与第三方广告

本站自身不会为登录或会话设置 cookie；语言偏好保存在浏览器本地，不是 cookie。本站可能展示由第三方广告网络（包括 **Google AdSense**）投放的广告，并按第 2 节所述使用 Google Analytics 4 进行匿名统计。

| 类型 | 用途 | 来源 | 管理方式 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 必要 | 页面交付、语言偏好（浏览器本地存储） | 本站 | 无法停用 |
| 统计 | 匿名访问统计 | Google Analytics 4 | 通过下方[「Cookie 设置」](#cookie-settings)同意或撤回 |
| 广告 | 个性化广告（仅在启用广告后） | Google AdSense / Google | [Google 广告设置](https://www.google.com/settings/ads/) |

- 包括 Google 在内的第三方供应商会使用 cookie，依据用户此前对本站点和／或其他网站的访问记录投放广告。
- Google 使用广告 cookie（包括 DART cookie），使其及其合作伙伴能够根据用户对本站点和其他网站的访问，投放基于兴趣的广告。
- 这些第三方可能在你的浏览器中读取或写入 cookie，并可能把本站访问信息与其他站点的数据结合。其数据处理遵循各自的隐私政策，我们无法控制。详见 [Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) 与 [Google 隐私权政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。
- 广告内容与编辑内容相互独立。本站发布的文章、设计与实测数据不会因广告合作而修改。

如果本站当前尚未启用广告，则本站不会设置广告 cookie；本节描述的是启用广告后适用的情形。

## 4. 你的选择

- 可在 [Google 广告设置](https://www.google.com/settings/ads/) 中停用 Google 的个性化广告。
- 可在 [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) 了解如何退出参与计划的第三方网络的兴趣广告；欧洲访客也可参考 [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)。
- 关于 Google 广告 cookie 的更多说明见 [Google 广告隐私页面](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)。
- 可随时通过下方[「Cookie 设置」](#cookie-settings)按钮开启或关闭统计 cookie，或安装 [Google Analytics 退出浏览器插件](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)。
- 需要修改之前的同意选择时，点同一个按钮即可：同意横幅会重新出现，新选择立即生效。
- 你也可以在浏览器设置中阻止或删除 cookie。阻止 cookie 不会影响本站正文内容的阅读。

## 5. 欧洲经济区、英国与瑞士访客的同意

本站全站使用 **Google 同意模式 v2（Consent Mode v2）**。对欧洲经济区、英国与瑞士的访客，`analytics_storage`、`ad_storage`、`ad_user_data` 与 `ad_personalization` 默认全部为 `denied`。只有在同意横幅中选择**「同意统计」**后才会写入统计或广告 cookie；选择**「仅必要」**则不会写入。在你做出选择之前，Google Analytics 可能仅收到不含 cookie、无法识别身份的匿名 ping。

可随时通过[「Cookie 设置」](#cookie-settings)或第 4 节中的按钮撤回或变更同意。日后若启用广告，个性化广告同样受该同意机制约束。本站的文章、设计与技术内容从不依赖广告或统计 cookie。

## 6. 保存期限与安全

邮件仅在回复咨询、保留技术协作记录所需的期间内保存，之后删除。本站不保存用户数据库。托管日志按托管方政策保留。统计数据为汇总数据，按我们 Google Analytics 媒体资源的保留设置保存；我们不会导出或保存个人级别的统计明细。互联网传输无法做到绝对安全，请不要通过邮件发送机密信息或敏感凭据。

## 7. 儿童隐私

本站面向工程师与硬件爱好者，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童曾与我们联系，请发邮件告知，我们会删除相关邮件。

文章中会链接到 GitHub 仓库、PDF 文档、YouTube 缩略图和图片托管等外部资源。这些服务有各自的隐私政策，在你点击链接或加载嵌入资源时可能收集数据，我们对其做法不承担责任。Google Analytics 4 是本站加载的唯一第三方脚本，详见第 2 节。

## 9. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权要求查询我们持有的信息、要求更正或删除，或反对某些处理。由于我们不保存用户数据库，此类请求通常只涉及邮件往来：请使用当初来信的邮箱发送至 [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com)。我们会在 30 天内回复。

## 10. 政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页并修改上方的「最后更新」日期。统计或广告 cookie 使用方式的重大变化也会在此说明。

## 11. 联系

对本政策的疑问请发至 [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com)，或使用[联系页面](/contact/)。另见[免责声明](/disclaimer/)与[授权与商店政策](/license/)。
