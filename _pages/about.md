## Better hardware for open source

bi4wms is an independent hardware engineering lab focused on making open-source projects easier to build and use, and on publishing practical electronics designs and engineering notes.

### What you'll find here

- **Open hardware projects:** circuit boards, design files, firmware links, and build instructions.
- **Engineering notes:** component selection, PCB layout, debugging records, measurements, and lessons learned.
- **Small-batch hardware:** design-file bundles, PCBs, kits, and assembled devices.
