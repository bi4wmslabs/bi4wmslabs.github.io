## Better hardware for open source

bi4wms is an independent hardware engineering lab focused on making open-source projects easier to build and use, and on publishing practical electronics designs and engineering notes.

### What you'll find here

- **Open hardware projects:** circuit boards, design files, firmware links, and build instructions.
- **Engineering notes:** component selection, PCB layout, debugging records, measurements, and lessons learned.
- **Small-batch hardware:** design-file bundles, PCBs, kits, and assembled devices.

### Open-source and paid products

Product pages should clearly state which files are open, which items are sold, and which licenses apply. Payment is handled by an external checkout provider; this website does not process or store card details.

### Contact

For enquiries, reach the lab at [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com).

## 为开源做更好的硬件

bi4wms 是一个独立的硬件工程实验室，专注于让开源项目更容易搭建和使用，并公开实用的电子设计与工程笔记。

### 这里有什么

- **开源硬件项目**：电路板、设计文件、固件链接与制作说明。
- **工程笔记**：器件选型、PCB 布局、调试记录、实测数据与经验教训。
- **小批量硬件**：设计文件包、PCB、套件与成品设备。

### 开源与付费产品

每个商品页都会明确说明哪些文件开放、哪些物品出售、以及适用的授权协议。付款由外部结算平台处理，本站不处理或保存银行卡信息。

咨询与合作请发邮件至 [bi4wms@gmail.com](mailto:bi4wms@gmail.com)。
