## Better hardware for open source

**BI4WMS Labs** is a personal technical blog and an independent hardware engineering lab, focused on making open-source projects easier to build and use, and on publishing practical electronics designs and engineering notes. The goal is clear, hands-on articles that help hardware enthusiasts and developers get started quickly.

### What you'll find here

- **Open hardware projects:** circuit boards, design files, firmware links, and build instructions.
- **Engineering notes:** component selection, PCB layout, debugging records, measurements, and lessons learned.
- **Small-batch hardware:** design-file bundles, PCBs, kits, and assembled devices.

### Topics

- ESP32 series development and applications
- KiCad schematic and PCB design, Gerber and drill files for manufacturing
- Sensors, low-power design, debugging techniques and practical tutorials
- IEPE data acquisition (OCTET DAQ001), STM32H7 firmware, LoRa sensor nodes and SimpleFOC motor control

## 为开源做更好的硬件

**BI4WMS Labs** 是个人技术博客，也是一个独立的硬件工程实验室，专注于让开源项目更容易搭建和使用，并公开实用的电子设计与工程笔记。目标是写出清晰、可上手实践的文章，帮助硬件爱好者与开发者快速入门。

### 这里有什么

- **开源硬件项目**：电路板、设计文件、固件链接与制作说明。
- **工程笔记**：器件选型、PCB 布局、调试记录、实测数据与经验教训。
- **小批量硬件**：设计文件包、PCB、套件与成品设备。

### 主题领域

- ESP32 系列开发与应用
- KiCad 原理图与 PCB 设计，以及 Gerber、钻孔等生产文件
- 传感器原理、低功耗设计、调试技巧与实用教程
- IEPE 数据采集（OCTET DAQ001）、STM32H7 固件、LoRa 传感器节点与 SimpleFOC 无刷电机控制

### 开源与付费产品
