## Licensing

Each project and product may use a different license. Always check the specific repository and product page. Purchasing a physical product or a packaged file bundle does not automatically transfer copyright or grant rights beyond the stated license.

## Digital products

Before publishing a paid download, state the exact files included, supported versions, known limitations, permitted use, and update policy. Explain how customers can contact you if a download fails.

## Physical products

Before accepting orders, publish accurate shipping regions, dispatch estimates, return/refund terms, warranty or defect-handling details, and any applicable product safety or compliance information.
